About LocumCalc

Empowering healthcare professionals with transparent compensation analysis and informed career decisions

Our Mission

To create transparency in healthcare staffing by providing professionals with the tools and insights they need to understand their true compensation value, make informed career decisions, and negotiate from a position of knowledge and strength.

Why We Built LocumCalc

Healthcare professionals dedicate their lives to caring for others, yet they often struggle to accurately assess their own compensation packages. The complexity of locum tenens contracts—with varying hourly rates, stipends, overtime calculations, and benefits—makes it nearly impossible to compare opportunities or understand true earning potential.

We saw brilliant physicians, nurses, and healthcare workers accepting contracts without fully understanding their worth, or spending hours manually calculating compensation details that should be transparent and immediate.

That's why we created LocumCalc: to level the playing field and give healthcare professionals the financial clarity they deserve.

Contract Transparency

Calculate true hourly rates including all stipends, benefits, and overtime. See exactly what each contract is worth in real, comparable terms.

Financial Planning

Understand your take-home pay with our paycheck calculator.

Market Intelligence

Compare your compensation against market averages for your specialty and location. Make informed decisions about your career path.

Privacy First

All calculations happen in your browser. We don't store or see your financial information. Your privacy is absolutely protected.
