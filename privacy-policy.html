LocumTrueRate Privacy Policy

At LocumTrueRate, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Calculator Data

When you use our Contract Calculator or Paycheck Calculator:

  • All calculations are performed locally in your browser
  • No calculation data is transmitted to our servers
  • Your financial information remains completely private
  • We do not store or have access to the values you enter

1.2 Account Information

If you choose to create an account (optional):

  • Email address
  • Name (optional)
  • Professional role (e.g., physician, nurse, etc.)
  • Hashed password (we never store plain text passwords)

1.3 Usage Data

We may collect anonymous usage statistics:

  • Pages visited
  • Features used
  • Browser type and version
  • General geographic location (country/state level)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide Services: Enable calculator functionality and saved calculations (if logged in)
  • Improve User Experience: Understand how our tools are used to make improvements
  • Communication: Send important updates about our services (only if you opt-in)
  • Security: Protect against fraudulent or unauthorized access

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure password hashing using bcrypt
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Limited access to user data (need-to-know basis only)
  • No storage of sensitive financial calculations

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect our rights and safety
  • With service providers who assist in operating our website (under strict confidentiality agreements)

5. Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for site functionality
  • Session Cookies: To maintain your login state
  • Preference Cookies: To remember your calculator settings

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work properly.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request removal of your account and data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from communications at any time

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for healthcare professionals and are not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@locumtruerate.com
  • Address: LocumTrueRate, Healthcare Division
    123 Medical Plaza, Suite 100
    Austin, TX 78701

10. California Privacy Rights

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). If you are a California resident, you may:

  • Request disclosure of personal information collected
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising privacy rights
