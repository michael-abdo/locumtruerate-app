Frequently Asked Questions

Is the calculator free to use?

Yes! Our contract and paycheck calculators are completely free. All calculations happen in your browser, so we don't store or see your financial information.

How accurate are the calculations?

Our calculators use industry-standard formulas and are regularly updated. However, tax calculations are estimates and you should consult a tax professional for official advice.

Can I save my calculations?

Currently, calculations are stored locally in your browser. We're working on account features to save and compare multiple contracts.

Do you support international contracts?

Our calculators are optimized for US healthcare contracts. We're exploring international support for future versions.

How do I report a bug?

Use the contact form above and select "Bug Report." Include what you were calculating and any error messages you saw.

Can I suggest new features?

Absolutely! We love hearing from healthcare professionals. Use the contact form and select "Feature Request" to share your ideas.
